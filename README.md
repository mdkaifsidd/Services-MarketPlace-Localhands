🛠️ Local Hands – Service Marketplace Platform

> **A Django-based service marketplace that connects customers with trusted local service providers.**

---

📖 About the Project

Local Hands is a web-based platform developed to make it easier for people to find and connect with local service providers in their area. Whether users need household services, repairs, or other professional assistance, the platform helps them discover the right provider quickly and conveniently.

For service providers, Local Hands offers a dedicated space to showcase their services, manage listings, and respond to customer requests, helping them expand their reach and grow their business.

The project was built with the aim of creating a simple, reliable, and user-friendly marketplace that benefits both customers and local professionals.

---

✨ Key Features

👤 Customer Features

* Create an account and securely log in.
* Browse a variety of available services.
* Search for nearby service providers.
* View detailed information about each service.
* Request or book services directly through the platform.
* Manage personal profile information.

 🔧 Service Provider Features

* Register as a service provider.
* Add, update, and manage service listings.
* Receive and respond to customer service requests.
* Maintain and edit provider profile information.

 🌐 Platform Features

* Secure user authentication.
* Responsive and easy-to-use interface.
* Organized service categories.
* Marketplace designed for smooth customer-provider interaction.
